Our approach

Work with nature's order, not against it.

Entropy is the drift towards disorder. Syntropy is the opposite: living systems that build complexity, fertility and resilience over time. That's what we design for.

Stratification

Plants are layered by height and light needs so every level of the canopy is productive.

Succession

Pioneer species prepare the ground for long-lived trees, just as a forest does on its own.

Pruning for soil

Strategic chop-and-drop feeds organic matter back to the soil and keeps water in the ground.
